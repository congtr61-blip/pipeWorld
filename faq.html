Pipe Product FAQs

Answers to common questions about PIPE WORLD's listed pipe products, project specifications, standards and requesting a quotation.

What pipe products can I enquire about from PIPE WORLD?

PIPE WORLD's website lists HDPE pressure pipe, U-PVC pressure pipe and electrical conduit. Contact the team to confirm current product availability and suitability for your project.

What information should I provide to confirm HDPE or U-PVC pipe specifications?

Share the intended application, required nominal diameter, pressure rating or SDR if specified, pipe length, applicable standard, quantity and delivery location. PIPE WORLD must confirm current specifications and availability.

Does PIPE WORLD provide SABS-certified pipes?

Applicable standards and certification depend on the specific product and project. Request the current technical datasheet and supporting compliance documents from PIPE WORLD; do not assume certification without confirming it for the selected product.

Can HDPE pipe be considered for water supply or irrigation projects?

PIPE WORLD lists HDPE pressure pipe for enquiries related to water conveyance, water networks and agricultural irrigation. Confirm the product's suitability and required pressure rating against the project design before specification or purchase.

How do I request a pipe quotation in Botswana?

Send PIPE WORLD your product type, project application, required sizes and ratings, quantity, applicable standard and delivery location using the homepage enquiry form or by emailing pipeworldbw@gmail.com. The team will confirm current availability and quotation details.

Need product-specific information?

Share your project requirements and the team can confirm current specifications and availability.
